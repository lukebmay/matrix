# interactive-play

**Rank:** 1

## Goal

An occasional ASCII portrait in the center slot, instead of a
saying. The old plan left this as a later, optional interlude
kind. Unit/Thread play, hover, and the saying playlist are
already in the product. This plan does not redo them.

One center slot. A saying and a portrait do not both paint.
The swap point is the unit boundary after the card hide, the
same place the saying content already changes. Hover on a
portrait follows the saying family: hasten reveal, do not
hasten hide, re-reveal on hide.
